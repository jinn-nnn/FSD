Agricultural Technology

Real machines and tools helping real farms.

No cartoons here — this page uses real-world photographs of agriculture technology and explains what each one does.

01
Drone Technology

Agricultural Drones

Drones can support crop monitoring and spraying. They can cover fields quickly and help farmers inspect crop conditions.

02
Water & Energy

Solar Irrigation

Solar-powered pumps can provide water for irrigation while reducing dependence on conventional electricity or fuel.

03
Controlled Growing

Smart Greenhouses

Automation can help control growing environments and improve the efficiency of protected cultivation.

04
Soil Monitoring

Soil Sensors

Moisture sensors provide measurements that can help farmers decide when irrigation is actually needed.

05
Precision Farming

GPS & Field Positioning

GPS and correction systems can guide agricultural machinery more accurately and reduce unnecessary overlap.

06
Farm Weather

Weather Stations

Weather stations measure conditions such as rainfall, temperature, wind and solar radiation for better farm planning.

The photographs on this page are real-world images from Wikimedia Commons. Licensing information is available on each original image page.

Technology With Purpose

Good technology turns information into better decisions.

Farmers can combine their experience with data from sensors, weather stations, GPS equipment and drones to use water and inputs more efficiently.
