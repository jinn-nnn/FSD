Our Crops

Real crops. Real fields. Real information.

Explore a wider selection of important crops with real-life photographs and easy-to-understand agricultural details.

01 • Oryza sativa

Rice (Paddy)

A major food crop that grows well in warm, water-rich conditions. It is widely cultivated across India.

Water: High • Use: Food grain

02 • Triticum aestivum

Wheat

An important cereal crop and a major source of carbohydrates and protein in many diets.

Season: Rabi • Use: Flour & food

03 • Zea mays

Maize

A versatile crop used for food, animal feed, starch and industrial products.

Water: Moderate • Use: Food & feed

04 • Gossypium spp.

Cotton

A major fibre crop. Cotton lint is processed into yarn and textiles.

Climate: Warm • Use: Fibre

05 • Saccharum spp.

Sugarcane

A tall, long-duration crop grown mainly for sugar and also used for ethanol and bioenergy.

Water: High • Use: Sugar & bioenergy

06 • Arachis hypogaea

Groundnut

An oilseed and food legume valued for protein, healthy fats and edible oil.

Season: Kharif/Summer • Use: Food & oil

07 • Glycine max

Soybean

A protein-rich oilseed crop used for food, animal feed and edible oil.

Season: Kharif • Use: Food & oil

08 • Cajanus cajan

Pigeon Pea (Tur Dal)

A major pulse crop that provides plant protein and improves farm diversity.

Season: Kharif • Use: Pulse

09 • Capsicum annuum

Chilli

An important spice crop grown for fresh and dried chilli production.

Climate: Warm • Use: Spice

10 • Solanum lycopersicum

Tomato

A widely cultivated vegetable crop valued for fresh consumption and processing.

Water: Moderate • Use: Food

11 • Musa spp.

Banana

A tropical fruit crop grown in warm regions and harvested throughout the year.

Climate: Tropical • Use: Fruit

12 • Curcuma longa

Turmeric

A rhizome crop widely grown for spice, food colouring and traditional uses.

Season: Kharif • Use: Spice
